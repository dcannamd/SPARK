## Position Summary

We are looking for an **Enablement Specialist** who is passionate about equipping our teams with the knowledge, tools, and resources they need to succeed. This role is focused on building and delivering high-impact enablement programs that drive measurable improvements in team performance and business outcomes.

You will partner closely with Sales, Customer Success, Product, and Marketing to identify gaps, design solutions, and deliver enablement that sticks. This is not just a training role — we expect this individual to operate with an **AI-forward mindset**, leveraging AI tools and automation to create more effective, scalable, and personalized learning experiences.

You will own key enablement initiatives across the customer lifecycle — from onboarding new hires to upskilling tenured team members on new products, messaging, and processes.

---

## Key Responsibilities

### Enablement Program Design & Delivery
- Design and deliver comprehensive enablement programs for Sales, Customer Success, and other revenue-facing teams
- Develop onboarding curricula for new hires that accelerate time to productivity
- Create ongoing training programs that keep teams updated on new products, features, messaging, and competitive positioning
- Build certification programs and learning paths that reinforce core skills and knowledge
- Deliver training through multiple formats including live sessions, self-paced learning, workshops, and hands-on practice
- Leverage AI tools to create more engaging, personalized, and scalable learning experiences

### Content Creation & Management
- Develop and maintain a library of enablement content including playbooks, battle cards, talk tracks, demo guides, and case studies
- Partner with Product Marketing to translate positioning and messaging into actionable sales and CS resources
- Work with subject matter experts to capture and codify best practices
- Use AI to streamline content creation, improve quality, and personalize resources for different audiences
- Maintain a centralized enablement hub that makes it easy for teams to find and use the resources they need

### Measurement & Continuous Improvement
- Define success metrics for enablement programs and track progress against goals
- Gather feedback from participants and stakeholders to continuously improve program quality
- Analyze data on content usage, training completion, and business outcomes to identify what's working and what's not
- Use insights to refine enablement programs and demonstrate ROI
- Build dashboards and reports that provide visibility into enablement effectiveness

### Cross-Functional Partnership
- Partner with Sales and CS leadership to understand business priorities and align enablement programs to key initiatives
- Collaborate with Product and Product Marketing to ensure teams are equipped to sell and support new launches
- Work with Recruiting and HR on onboarding and new hire ramp programs
- Build strong relationships with team members to understand their needs and challenges

### AI-Forward Mindset
- Proactively identify opportunities to leverage AI and automation to improve enablement workflows and outcomes
- Test and implement new AI tools for content creation, training delivery, and performance management
- Share best practices and learnings with the broader team to elevate our overall approach to enablement
- Stay current on emerging enablement technologies and methodologies

## What Success Looks Like

- New hires ramp faster and hit productivity milestones ahead of schedule
- Teams consistently demonstrate strong product knowledge, messaging fluency, and selling skills
- Enablement content is actively used and rated highly by participants
- Sales and CS leadership report that enablement programs are directly contributing to better performance
- Enablement programs scale efficiently without proportional increases in effort or cost
- Clear metrics demonstrate the ROI of enablement initiatives
