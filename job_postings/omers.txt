## About the Role

Reporting to the Manager, Learning & Development, we are seeking a highly skilled **Lead, Learning & Development** to drive the design and delivery of impactful learning experiences across our organization. In this role, you will serve as a subject matter expert in learning design strategy, collaborate cross-functionally with stakeholders, and lead complex, high-visibility projects that support employee development and organizational priorities. You'll design and implement blended learning programs, develop learning materials, track the effectiveness of our training, and support our learning systems.

## Responsibilities

### Instructional & Learning Design Expertise
- Design and develop high-impact learning solutions, including eLearning modules, instructor-led training (ILT), virtual instructor-led training (vILT), microlearning, job aids, and performance support tools
- Design, develop and consult on engaging blended learning experiences using formats such as videos, e-learning modules, articles, and job aids
- Create mock-ups, scripts, or storyboards for learning materials, including videos, e-learning modules, articles, job aids, and learning environments
- Apply established instructional design frameworks (e.g., ADDIE, SAM) grounded in adult learning principles to drive measurable outcomes

### Project & Program Leadership
- Manage L&D projects from concept through delivery, including scope definition, timelines, quality control, and stakeholder engagement
- Lead the intake process for training projects, ensuring thorough understanding of requirements and aligning resources and timelines to meet project goals
- Support the implementation, administration and maintenance of L&D activities through Workday LMS, including course/campaign logistics and distribution

### Quality, Standards & Innovation
- Champion best practices in learning experience design, accessibility, user engagement, and learner-centered methodologies
- Maintain design consistency across learning products via media assets, templates, standards, and review processes
- Identify opportunities for innovation in learning design and technology; pilot new tools and approaches
- Partner effectively with HR Business Partners and other HR Centers of Excellence (COEs) to ensure alignment and maximize impact

### Evaluation & Continuous Improvement
- Track and report on training activities, using data to assess program effectiveness and impact, and inform continuous improvement and strategic planning

### Learning Innovation & AI Integration
- Collaborate with L&D technology partners to integrate AI-enabled solutions into the learning tech ecosystem (e.g., search, recommendations, content tagging)
- Lead internal knowledge sharing and upskilling initiatives related to emerging AI trends in instructional design and learning delivery

## What You Bring

- 3+ years' experience in a Learning or related HR role, with solid experience designing and developing adult learning programs, preferably in corporate or financial services
- Deep knowledge of adult learning theory, performance-based design, and modern workplace learning trends
- Strong command of instructional design tools (e.g., Articulate Storyline, Rise, Adobe Creative Suite, Vyond) and productivity tools (e.g., Scribe, VEED, Mentimeter)
- Experience with Learning Management Systems, preferably Workday Learning and Sana
- Adaptable, with a continuous improvement mindset and ability to learn quickly in a changing environment
- Ability to multi-task and prioritize multiple initiatives and deadlines while meeting SLAs
- Ability to independently plan, organize, prioritize and execute multiple activities to meet business objectives
- Excellent organizational and time management skills with high attention to detail
- Strong communication skills (listening, verbal, written, presentation prep and delivery), including with senior stakeholders
- Fully proficient in Microsoft Office (Word, PowerPoint, Teams, SharePoint)
