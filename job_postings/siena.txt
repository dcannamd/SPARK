# Head of Education & Enablement — Siena

## About Siena

Siena is the **Agent of Record** for consumer brands — one agent that owns the entire customer journey, running on a shared intelligence layer no point tool can replicate.

Consumer brands run ten or more disconnected tools to manage their customers. Siena collapses that stack into a single agent. It started with support (the hardest surface a brand has) and expands from there: Shopping Agent, Social Agent, Siena working alongside a brand's own operators. One agent across every surface, with shared memory, shared context, and shared tools.

Hundreds of brands already run real customer volume on Siena in production. This is built, working, and growing — and we are racing to build what comes next.

## The Role

Siena is the CX operating system for brands. We ship faster than anyone in the category — and that pace has outrun our ability to teach what we're building.

This role exists to change that. You will teach Siena to three audiences:

1. **The people who build it** — internal team
2. **The brands who use it** — customers
3. **The market that will eventually adopt it** — the broader world

You will build the curriculum, the community, and the credentials that make Siena something people *learn*, not something they *discover*.

> You are a product person who teaches. A builder who writes. A communicator who ships.

---

## What You'll Do

- **Product education** — Every release lands with a session, a doc, and a demo the company actually walks away knowing.
- **Customer workshops** — Recurring product sessions, one use case at a time. Real depth, real outcomes.
- **Customer onboarding** — From signed contract to first value, repeatable across every segment.
- **New hire onboarding** — Ramp every new hire through your program so they're productive faster.
- **Demo library** — Live, recorded, vertical-specific, persona-specific. Sales pulls from it. Customers learn from it.
- **Siena Academy** — Curriculum, certifications, learning paths. Internal and external. The credential brands earn to show they know Siena.
- **Siena community** — Recurring spaces, events, and rituals where customers, partners, and operators learn from us and each other.
- **Market education** — Open courses, public content, frameworks others adopt. The Academy becomes the category-defining resource on CX AI.
- **Sales enablement** — Demo scripts, vertical storylines, the words that close deals.
- **AI systems** — Build the agents that scale all of the above. Your taste is the final filter.
