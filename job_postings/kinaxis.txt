# Director, Product Education & Learning Programs
**Kinaxis** | Ottawa & Toronto, Canada (Hybrid) | Other Canadian Locations (Remote)

---

## About Kinaxis

Kinaxis is a global leader in modern supply chain orchestration, powering complex global supply chains and supporting the people who manage them. Founded in 1984 by a team of three engineers, Kinaxis has grown to over 2,000 employees across 6 global offices, with headquarters in Ottawa, Canada.

Their AI-infused platform provides full transparency and visibility across end-to-end supply chains, enabling faster, better decisions. Trusted by renowned global brands — including Lockheed Martin, Unilever, P&G, ExxonMobil, and Cisco — Kinaxis serves more than 40,000 users in over 100 countries.

---

## About the Role

The Director, Product Education & Learning Programs is responsible for the strategy, creation, delivery, and continuous improvement of customer- and partner-facing learning programs that enable successful product adoption, implementation, and value realization.

The incumbent will lead teams responsible for end-to-end delivery of learning programs — including curriculum development, training delivery, program operations, and partner learning programs — ensuring offerings are scalable, consistent, and tightly aligned to product release cycles and lifecycle milestones.

The Director works closely with Product, Sales, Customer Success, and Partner organizations to ensure learning programs directly support customer adoption, implementation success, and long-term product value.

---

## Responsibilities

- Define and lead the strategy for customer and partner learning and enablement programs, ensuring alignment with product roadmap, go-to-market priorities, and customer success objectives.
- Oversee the full lifecycle of customer- and partner-facing learning programs, including curriculum design and development, certification pathways, and ongoing updates.
- Ensure training programs are embedded into product release cycles, enabling timely and effective customer and partner readiness for new and enhanced capabilities.
- Lead the design and execution of scalable training delivery models, including instructor-led, virtual, self-paced learning experiences, and bootcamps.
- Own partner learning programs, ensuring partners are equipped with the knowledge and skills required to successfully implement, support, and extend product value.
- Establish and manage program operations, including planning, scheduling, governance, and delivery coordination across global audiences.
- Working with finance, manage the commercial model associated with customer and partner-related training offerings.
- Work directly with Product teams to translate product functionality and roadmap changes into effective learning content and delivery.
- Collaborate with key stakeholders across Sales, Customer Success, Support, and Partner teams to ensure learning programs support adoption, implementation success, and customer outcomes.
- Define and track key performance indicators such as adoption, engagement, completion, and effectiveness; use insights to continuously improve programs.
- Prepare and present program updates to senior leadership, including performance, impact, risks, and opportunities.
- Oversee the management of external vendors, contractors, and platform partners as needed to support scalable program delivery.
